COMPANY POLICIES

LEAVE POLICY

Employees receive 24 paid leaves per year.

Employees can take a maximum of 2 consecutive sick leaves without submitting a medical certificate.

Employees should submit planned leave requests at least 3 working days in advance.

Emergency leave should be communicated to the manager as soon as possible.

Unused paid leaves can be carried forward to the next year up to a maximum of 10 days.


WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.

Employees must obtain approval from their manager before working from home.

Employees working from home must remain available during normal working hours.

Important meetings should be attended regardless of whether the employee is working from the office or remotely.


EXPENSE POLICY

Employees can claim approved business expenses.

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

Employees must provide valid receipts for expenses.

Travel expenses require manager approval.

Personal expenses are not reimbursable.


IT SECURITY POLICY

Employees must not share their company passwords with anyone.

Company laptops must be protected with a password or other approved authentication method.

Employees must report suspected security incidents to the IT team immediately.

Company data must not be copied to unauthorized personal storage.


EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

Employees are eligible for health insurance according to the company's benefits plan.

Employees may participate in the company's professional development programs.

Employees should contact the HR department for questions about benefits and eligibility.
